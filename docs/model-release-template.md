## 3. Grant of rights

The Producer grants the Client a **perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide,
non-exclusive, royalty-free licence** to use, reproduce, adapt, edit, composite,
distribute and display the photographs, and the Producer's likeness within them,
for any purpose connected with the Client's business — including on websites,
in advertising, in printed material, and in products sold or given away.

This includes the right to **modify the images**, including compositing the
Producer's likeness into photographs of other people's homes, cropping,
resizing, colour grading and adding animation or other visual effects.

## Photographer: note before returning

This release is only worth anything if it is actually signed. A delivery of
images with an unsigned release is a liability, not an asset — and the risk here
is specifically that the likeness ends up in a product shown to children, where
image rights are scrutinised most closely.

If the Santa is played by anyone other than the photographer themselves, get a
release from **that person**, not from the photographer.
