🎅Santa
Put Santa where you want him. This is a photograph, not a film — he does not walk anywhere, you place him. Drag 🎅 to where he should sit, then stretch the yellow ruler 📏 → 📐 up something real in your own photo and say what it is above. His size is then measured from your furniture instead of guessed, so he sits in the room rather than floating in it.

Your photo stays on your phone

If you are under 18, here's the short version: your photo never leaves this device. It never gets sent anywhere. We don't see it, we don't keep it, and when you close this tab it's gone. Nothing here asks for your name, your address or anything about you.

For grown-ups. This tool runs entirely inside your browser. Your photo is decoded into the page's memory, drawn on, and released when you close the tab or press Forget my photo. It is never uploaded, never stored on a server, and never seen by us or anyone else.

  • No account, no login, no email address
  • No cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking
  • No third-party scripts on this page — enforced by a build check
  • Face detection is optional and runs on your device; no face data is stored or sent anywhere
  • You are responsible for only using photos you have the right to use

Questions, or something to report?

Write to atiff.khan@yahoo.com with the subject "Privacy". We will answer within 5 working days. You do not need to give us your name, and you can ask us to delete anything you think we hold — the honest answer is usually that we hold nothing, and we will tell you.

Full assessment: see docs/dpia-santas-visit.md in the project. This is a free tool with no advertising and no data collection.

Nothing here can tell a sofa arm from a cushion in a photograph, so you paint it. Turn this on and brush over anything that stands between Santa and the camera — the table edge, a chair back, a cushion — and the real pixels are laid back over him. It is the single thing that stops him looking stuck on top of your furniture.
